Bank Marketing Analytics Dashboard 

📌 Project Overview

The Bank Marketing Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project developed to analyze customer responses, campaign performance, loan distribution, and subscription trends in bank marketing campaigns.

This dashboard helps financial institutions understand customer behavior, evaluate marketing effectiveness, and improve decision-making for future campaigns.

🎯 Problem Statement

Banks conduct marketing campaigns to promote financial products and increase customer subscriptions. However, analyzing customer responses, campaign calls, and demographic factors manually can be difficult and time-consuming.

The objective of this project was to transform raw banking marketing data into an interactive dashboard that provides meaningful insights into customer engagement, loan distribution, and campaign performance.

📈 Key Insights

The dashboard recorded 125K total campaign calls.
Customers have an average account balance of 1.36K.
The overall subscription rate is 45.21K.
Customers with secondary education represent the highest loan distribution among education categories.
Subscription distribution is nearly balanced across different marital status categories.
Monthly analysis shows variations in average call duration, helping identify periods of higher customer engagement.
Different customer categories contribute equally to overall campaign distribution.
